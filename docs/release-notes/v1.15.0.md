This proposed release carries the licensing transition from v1.14.0. Do not tag or publish it until the release version, notices, and package artifacts have been reviewed.

- License: source-available under the MIT License subject to the Commons Clause License Condition v1.0; not OSI open source. Read the complete [`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE) and [`commercial licensing notes`](../../COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md). Ordinary company/internal work and freelance work using the tool to deliver other work are permitted subject to the exact license. Separate written permission is required before selling to third parties a product or service whose value derives entirely or substantially from claudex-switch functionality, including related hosting or consulting/support. This is not a blanket ban on commercial integration; a genuinely value-added larger product may remain permitted under the clause.
- Transition: public v1.14.0 retains its prior MIT declaration and any rights it may have granted, including for future recipients of that historical release. This change is prospective and does not retag or rewrite past releases. A missing root `LICENSE` in v1.14.0 does not by itself prove there was no license grant.
- Distribution: the new package and each platform release archive include the combined `LICENSE` and `COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md`; Homebrew installs both notices with the binary. The MIT + Commons Clause combination has no SPDX identifier, so future generated formula metadata uses Homebrew's supported `:cannot_represent` value.
- Documentation: clarify that OpenCode's `--auto` flag approves permissions not explicitly denied, and that `/connect` in a normal shared OpenCode session may rewrite the global auth file; use `claudex-switch add` or `refresh` to change Go credentials.

No runtime account-switching behavior is changed by this proposed release.
